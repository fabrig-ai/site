Reliable

  • Effects are made once.

    Each kind of write has a recovery class. An uncertain one waits for a person, not a guess.

  • Merging is pinned to an exact commit.

    The head its decision saw, and no other.

Scalable

  • Scales out with workers.

    Workers dial one engine. High availability is planned on skein-kv, a replicated single-writer store.

  • The target: landed in minutes.

    A ready branch lands on its first attempt, within minutes.

Flexible

  • Your process is configuration.

    Templates, charters and landing rules are settings, on jig’s model of tasks and delegation.

  • Delegated authority only narrows.

    Budgets are carved from the parent’s.
